I. The Things That Instantly Remind Me of You

  • The smell of Peri-Peri fries: Even though I still maintain they are a culinary mistake and an absolute crime against potatoes, seeing them on any menu instantly brings a smirk to my face because I know you'd order them without hesitation.
  • Open paperbacks and quiet reading corners: Seeing someone absorbed in a thick book or talking about character psychology immediately triggers thoughts of your bookish side and the way you disappear into stories.
  • Psychology articles and behavioral quirks: Whenever someone breaks down why humans act the way they do, or when someone analyzes everyday habits, I think of how much you love understanding the human mind.

  • Your specific brand of "Kind, Funny, and Crazy": You have a genuine kindness that makes people feel safe around you, paired with an effortless wit that catches people off-guard, topped off with a chaotic, completely unhinged side that makes everyday life ten times more entertaining.
  • Your curiosity about the human mind: I love that you don't just observe life on the surface; your fascination with psychology and reading means you look deeper into people, understand emotions, and carry a thoughtful perspective on everything.
  • Your meme fluency: In a world where people communicate in long, exhausting conversations, I love that our humor operates on the exact same wavelength. A single ridiculous reel or an inside joke says more between us than an hour-long chat.

III. Why I Like You & Why I Am Grateful for You
